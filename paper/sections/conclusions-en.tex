\section{Conclusions}
\label{conclusions:section}

For every $n\ge5$, the local tetrahedra of the reference EJM can expand or contract continuously without rotating. At twice the reference radius, the trace distance between any two distinct one-qubit marginals at a given site has doubled, yet every basis state remains genuinely $n$-partite entangled. At each radius on the path, every state attains the largest genuine multipartite concurrence compatible with its one-qubit polarization.

For even $n\ge6$, the new and cosine paths start at the same measurement and both reach zero radius. Every one-qubit marginal is then $I/2$, but for every outcome $C_{\rm GME}=1$ at the new endpoint and $C_{\rm GME}<1$ at the cosine endpoint. For each fixed even $n\ge6$, this strict gap persists at sufficiently small positive radii, where corresponding outcomes have identical, nonzero one-qubit Bloch vectors.
