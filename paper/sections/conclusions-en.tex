\section{Conclusions}
\label{conclusions:section}

For every $n\ge5$, the local tetrahedra of the reference EJM can expand or contract continuously without rotating. Doubling their radius doubles the trace distance between any two distinct one-qubit marginals at a given site, yet every basis state remains genuinely $n$-partite entangled. More generally, at each radius on the path, every state attains the largest genuine multipartite concurrence compatible with its one-qubit polarization.

For each $n\ge5$, the unified path and its cosine counterpart start at the same reference measurement. At zero radius, every one-qubit marginal is $I/2$, and every outcome has $C_{\rm GME}=1$ at the unified endpoint and $C_{\rm GME}<1$ at the cosine endpoint. For each fixed $n$, this strict gap persists at sufficiently small positive radii, where corresponding outcomes have identical, nonzero one-qubit Bloch vectors.
