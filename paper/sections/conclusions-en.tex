\section{Conclusions}
\label{conclusions:section}

We have constructed a continuous family of elegant joint measurements for every integer $n\ge5$, with fixed local tetrahedral directions and common radius covering $[0,2r_E(n)]$ through the specified reference measurement. The upper endpoint is attained and optimal among all admissible straight phase paths with two-valued real generators in the fixed phase representation. A single four-feature mechanism thus gives continuous control of local polarization while preserving global orthogonality. The embedding of the radius-increasing and known cosine families for even $n\ge8$, together with the radius increase along a curved path at $n=4$, further shows how compatible phase directions and the shape of the phase path determine the available deformations.

The main mathematical results of this paper have been formally proved in Lean.
